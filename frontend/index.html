FERRIX · SOCIAL POST RELAY

Information travels. Independently.

Ferrix turns public social posts into standalone images you can download and share anywhere. The message outlives the messenger.

How it works

  1. Paste the URL of a public X post. Optionally include the author's own thread.
  2. Adjust the card in the editor: theme (Paper, Carbon, Ferrix), card width, attribution (Original, Minimal, Clean), timestamp, engagement metrics, censored @username, platform mark.
  3. For threads: reorder cards, pick which posts to include, combine them into one image or export individual numbered cards.
  4. Download as PNG or WebP at 1×, 2× or 3×. Multi-image exports come as a ZIP with a sources.json crediting the original posts.

Works with public X and Threads posts. No account required. Images are rendered on your device.
